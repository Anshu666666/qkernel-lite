EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Project in Five Incontrovertible Facts

Empirical Capstone Finding

1. Clinical Testing Ground: We tested whether Projected Quantum Kernels (PQK) improve classification of pediatric chest X-rays when only 10 to 100 labeled radiographs exist.

2. The Hybrid Pipeline: Frozen ResNet-18 features (512-d) are reduced by train-fold PCA to 8 components, angle-encoded into an 8-qubit circuit, and projected to 24 Pauli expectation values.

3. The Scientific Control: An identical RBF kernel on those 24 values feeds the exact same convex dual SVM as the classical baseline (M2), isolating the feature map.

4. The Empirical Reality: The quantum kernel stayed below the classical kernel at every N tested (M3 − M2 = −0.030 to −0.124 AUC, p < 0.001), and multi-qubit entanglement degraded scores further.

5. The Classical Equivalent: The only quantum circuit that matched the classical control within ±0.02 AUC is a 1-layer unentangled product state, which is trivially classically simulable.

DAKSH SINGH · DATA & SCARCITY

Pediatric Radiography & Extreme Label Scarcity

Dataset & Sampling Methodology

PneumoniaMNIST Protocol

Clinical Context: Pediatric chest radiographs from Kermany et al. (Cell 2018), downsampled to 28×28 grayscale. Normal vs. bacterial/viral pneumonia.

Fixed Evaluation Set: 624 test images (234 normal, 390 pneumonia). The test set was untouched during hyperparameter tuning and touched exactly once.

Simulated Scarcity Regimes:

  • N = 10 images (5 normal, 5 pneumonia)
  • N = 20 images (10 normal, 10 pneumonia)
  • N = 50 images (25 normal, 25 pneumonia)
  • N = 100 images (50 normal, 50 pneumonia)

Sampling Integrity & Prevalence Invariance

Strict Stratification: Each subset is sampled with an exact 50/50 class balance. A fixed seed defines the exact patient IDs sampled.

Why ROC-AUC over Accuracy/F1?

  • Training data is artificially balanced (50% prevalence), whereas the test set is 62.5% pneumonia.
  • Accuracy and F1 change arbitrarily with class prevalence and decision threshold.
  • ROC-AUC measures the probability that a random positive patient scores higher than a random negative patient—independent of threshold or prevalence.

DAKSH SINGH · EVALUATION & STATISTICS

Paired Student's t-Test & Bonferroni Family-Wise Control

Hypothesis Testing & Equivalence Bounds

Paired Design (15 Seeds, df = 14)

Why Paired? Testing M1, M2, and M3 on the exact same patient subsets (seeds 0 to 14) eliminates between-subset variance, isolating model differences.

Why Student's t and NOT z-score? With n = 15 sample runs, population variance is unknown. The sample standard deviation s requires Student's t distribution with df = 14 (t_crit = 2.145 for 95% CI).

CI_95% = Δ̄ ± t_{0.025, 14} · (s_Δ / √15)

Strict Bonferroni Correction: Across 12 primary statistical comparisons, family-wise significance requires:

α_bonf = 0.05 / 12 = 0.00417

Equivalence Testing Bound (±0.02 AUC)

Absence of Evidence ≠ Evidence of Absence: A high p-value alone does not prove two models are equal. Equivalence requires the entire 95% confidence interval of the difference to fall inside a predefined clinical bound [−0.02, +0.02] AUC.

Equivalence Gate: CI_95%(M_test − M_ref) ⊆ [−0.02, +0.02]

Verification: At N = 20, 50, 100, the 1-layer unentangled product state passes this equivalence test against M2. Entangled circuits fail it at every N.

ANSHUMAN BISWAS · CLASSICAL PIPELINE

M1 Scratch CNN & Frozen ResNet-18 Transfer Learning

Feature Extraction & Overfitting Mechanics

M1: Scratch CNN (105,826 Weights)

Architecture: 2 Conv layers (16 and 32 filters, 3×3 kernel) + ReLU + MaxPool + Linear(32×7×7, 64) + Linear(64, 1).

Parameter Under-Determination: Training 105k parameters on N = 10 samples (P/N > 10,000). The network easily memorizes training instances.

Why Did M1 Not Collapse to Chance? M1 achieves 0.847 AUC at N=10 because PneumoniaMNIST has prominent global lung opacity differences that a simple filter can detect. We report what we measured: M1 does surprisingly well, so we drop claims that 'M1 completely fails'.

Frozen ResNet-18 Transfer Learning

Why Freeze 11.7M Parameters? Fine-tuning 11 million weights on 10 images guarantees severe overfitting. Freezing the backbone means no medical image updates convolutional filters; it acts solely as a fixed nonlinear feature extractor.

Feature Pipeline: Grayscale repeated to 3 channels → ImageNet normalization → ResNet-18 in eval() mode (BatchNorm uses stored population statistics) → Global Average Pooling yields a rich 512-dimensional vector per radiograph.

ANSHUMAN BISWAS · CLASSICAL BASELINE (M2)

PCA Dimensionality Reduction & Classical Control (M2)

Compression Trade-Offs & Dual Convex Optimization

Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

Why PCA? Quantum simulation is limited to 8 qubits. We compress 512 features to 8 orthogonal components.

Zero Data Leakage: PCA is fitted strictly on the N training samples of each fold. At N = 10, the maximum rank is N − 1 = 9.

What is Lost in 504 Directions? 8 components capture ~75% cumulative variance. The remaining 504 directions contain fine bone texture, scanner noise, and subtle opacities.

Min-Max Scaling: Scaled to [0, π] for quantum rotation compatibility, clipping test values to [0, π].

M2: Classical RBF-SVM (The True Control)

Why M2 is the Control: M2 uses the identical 8 PCA features, identical [0, π] scaling, and identical dual convex C-SVM solver as M3. Only the kernel function differs.

k_{M2}(x, x') = \exp(-\gamma ||x - x'||^2)

Performance Lift: M2 beats M1 by:

  • N = 10: +0.049 AUC (p = 0.044)
  • N = 20: +0.045 AUC (p = 0.0010, survives Bonferroni)
  • N = 50: +0.021 AUC (p = 0.0032, survives Bonferroni)
  • N = 100: +0.014 AUC (p = 0.038)

HIMMAT SIRWEE · QUANTUM CIRCUIT

8-Qubit Angle Encoding & Hardware Circuit Topology

Bloch Sphere Representation & Entangling Gates

Angle Encoding Mechanics

The Register: 8 qubits initialized in state |00000000⟩. Each PCA component θ_i is assigned to one qubit.

Single-Qubit Gate Sequence: On each qubit i:

R_Y(s\theta_i) = \exp(-i \frac{s\theta_i}{2} Y), \quad R_Z(s\theta_i) = \exp(-i \frac{s\theta_i}{2} Z)

Entangling Ring: Circular CNOT chain: (0,1), (1,2), ..., (6,7), (7,0). Correlates adjacent qubits across the register.

Re-Uploading: In 2-layer circuits, the feature rotations and CNOT rings are repeated twice.

Exact Simulation vs. Physical Hardware

Exact Statevector (PennyLane lightning.qubit): We simulate state vectors exactly without shot noise. Statevector dimension = 2^8 = 256 complex amplitudes.

What Real NISQ Hardware Would Face:

  • Finite shot sampling error: standard error ≈ 1 / √shots.
  • Gate infidelity and thermal relaxation (T1, T2 dephasing).
  • QPU routing overhead for circular CNOT topology.

Our simulation shows that the deficit exists in pure physics, even with zero hardware noise.

HIMMAT SIRWEE · MATHEMATICAL DERIVATION

Analytical Derivation: 1-Layer Product State & Small-s Duality

Closed-Form Bloch Vector & Classical Equivalence

Exact Closed Form (1-Layer, Unentangled)

Applying R_Y(sθ) to |0⟩ rotates the state in the X-Z plane:

|\psi_1\rangle = \cos(s\theta/2)|0\rangle + \sin(s\theta/2)|1\rangle

Applying R_Z(sθ) adds a relative phase:

|\psi\rangle = \cos(s\theta/2)|0\rangle + e^{is\theta}\sin(s\theta/2)|1\rangle

Calculating expectation values ⟨P⟩ = ⟨ψ|P|ψ⟩ for Pauli operators:

⟨X⟩ = \sin(s\theta)\cos(s\theta), \quad ⟨Y⟩ = \sin^2(s\theta), \quad ⟨Z⟩ = \cos(s\theta)

Key Insight: ⟨Y⟩ is non-zero because R_Z rotates the state off the X-Z meridian onto the Bloch sphere body.

Small-s Linearization (Classical Duality)

When scale factor s is tuned small (e.g. s = 0.0625 or 0.125), Taylor expansion around zero gives:

⟨X⟩ \approx s\theta, \quad ⟨Y⟩ \approx (s\theta)^2, \quad ⟨Z⟩ \approx 1 - \frac{(s\theta)^2}{2}

The Classical Duality:

  • The quantum features become strictly linear in ⟨X⟩ and quadratic in ⟨Y⟩ and ⟨Z⟩.
  • An RBF kernel on these expectation values reduces to a standard classical polynomial/RBF kernel.
  • M3 matches M2 at small s precisely because the quantum circuit is forced to act as a smooth classical map!

HIMMAT SIRWEE · PHYSICAL MECHANICS

Physical Failure Modes: Phase Wrapping & Entanglement Collapse

Why Default Encoding (s = 1) and CNOT Gates Degrade Performance

Phase Wrapping Turnaround (s = 1.00)

The Periodicity Bug: In a 2-layer circuit, rotation angles accumulate. When θ spans [0, π], the effective phase exceeds π.

Because U(π)²|0⟩ = |0⟩ (up to global phase), ⟨Z⟩ reaches −1 at θ = π/2 and then turns around, returning to +1 at θ = π.

⟨Z(\theta = 0)⟩ = +1 \quad \text{and} \quad ⟨Z(\theta = \pi)⟩ = +1

Diagnostic Disaster: Completely healthy lungs (low opacity, θ ≈ 0) and severely consolidated pneumonia lungs (high opacity, θ ≈ π) are mapped to identical quantum expectation values!

Entanglement Shortens the Bloch Vector

Reduced Density Matrices: When qubits are entangled by CNOTs, individual qubits enter mixed states described by partial traces:

\rho_i = \text{Tr}_{\bar{i}}(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|), \quad ||\mathbf{r}_i||_2 < 1

For a maximally entangled Bell pair, ⟨X⟩ = ⟨Y⟩ = ⟨Z⟩ = 0 (the center of the sphere).

Empirical Variance Collapse: In our 8Q register at s = 1.00:

  • Unentangled feature variance: 0.290
  • Entangled feature variance: 0.0085 (34× collapse!)

CNOT gates crush the discriminative dynamic range of single-qubit projections.

SAWAN KUMAR · KERNEL THEORY

Projected Quantum Kernels vs. Fidelity Barren Plateaus

Mercer Condition & Hilbert Space Concentration

Global Fidelity Kernels Fail in High Dims

Fidelity Definition: k_fid(x, x') = |⟨φ(x)|φ(x')⟩|². For two single-qubit Bloch vectors a and b, k_fid = (1 + a·b)/2.

Exponential Concentration: In an n-qubit Hilbert space (2^n dimensions), Haar-random states become orthogonal with high probability. Kernel values concentrate around 0:

\text{Var}_{x, x'}[k_{\text{fid}}(x, x')] \sim \mathcal{O}(2^{-n})

For large n, the Gram matrix collapses to the identity matrix I, rendering classification impossible.

Projected Quantum Kernels (Huang et al. 2021)

Projection Mechanism: Instead of computing global Hilbert overlaps, PQK projects the quantum state back to classical metric space via 1-particle reduced density matrices:

E(x) = [\langle X_1 \rangle, \langle Y_1 \rangle, \langle Z_1 \rangle, \dots, \langle Z_n \rangle]^T \in \mathbb{R}^{3n}
k_{\text{PQK}}(x, x') = \exp(-\gamma \sum_{k=1}^{3n} (E_k(x) - E_k(x'))^2)

Computational Advantage: Requires only 1 circuit evaluation per image (not per image-pair). Avoids global exponential concentration.

SAWAN KUMAR · DIAGNOSTICS & ZZ ABLATION

Empirical Diagnostics & The Tested ZZ Correlator Hypothesis

Distance Spread, Spearman Rank & Observable Sets

Quantitative Kernel Diagnostics

Off-Diagonal Spread (std/median): Measures whether kernel entries distinguish between pairs or collapse to a constant. M3 default has severe concentration when untuned.

Input-Space Spearman Rank Correlation: Evaluates whether Euclidean distance in PCA space maps monotonically to kernel distance.

  • M2 Classical RBF: Rank correlation = 0.998
  • M3 1-Layer Unentangled: Rank correlation = 0.924
  • M3 2-Layer Entangled: Rank correlation = 0.412 (severe distortion!)

The Refuted ZZ Correlator Hypothesis

The Hypothesis: Did entangled circuits perform poorly because 1-qubit Paulis discarded two-qubit quantum correlations created by CNOT gates?

The Experiment: Added 8 nearest-neighbor ⟨Z_i Z_{i+1}⟩ observables (expanding to 32 expectations) on 8Q 2L Ent across 5 seeds.

The Outcome: Adding ZZ correlators decreased test AUC at every sample size: N=10 dropped −0.013; N=20 dropped −0.034; N=50 dropped −0.010; N=100 dropped −0.014.

Honest Verdict: Adding two-qubit correlators did not help. The exact cause of the entanglement deficit remains unexplained.

CONCLUSION & VIVA DEFENSE

Bounded Conclusions & Viva Defense Integrity

Scientific Honesty Over Hype

What We Stand Behind (Permissible)

  • Pretrained Transfer Learning: Accounts for 100% of diagnostic capability (+0.36 AUC lift over scratch CNN at N=10).
  • No Quantum Advantage: M3 is statistically detectably below M2 across all sample sizes (deficits of −0.030 to −0.124 AUC).
  • Product State Equivalence: The only quantum model matching M2 within ±0.02 AUC is a classically simulable product state.
  • Entanglement Hurts: Circular CNOT entangling gates collapse single-qubit expectation variance from 0.29 to 0.0085.

What We Explicitly Do Not Claim

  • We do not claim a general proof that quantum kernels cannot classify medical images.
  • We do not claim entanglement scrambles pathology in all architectures.
  • We do not claim M3 'ties' or 'matches' M2.
  • We report what we measured with full transparency, 960 runs, 15 seeds, and zero data leakage.
A rigorous null result is superior to an unreproducible claim.
