Capstone inquiry in six pillars.
Rigorous Methodology · Matched Protocols
01.

The M1 Fallacy

Scratch CNNs fail at small N (0.536 AUC at N=10), creating a fraudulent illusion of quantum supremacy if used as the sole baseline.

02.

Classical Transfer (M2)

Pretrained ImageNet ResNet-18 with PCA-8 compression and tuned RBF SVM achieves 0.896 AUC at N=10 and 0.965 AUC at N=100.

03.

Quantum Kernel (M3)

Hardware-efficient angle encoding circuits evaluated via exact statevector Pauli-Z, X, Y expectation value vectors.

04.

15-Seed Monte Carlo

Exact identical seeds (0–14) and 5-fold Stratified CV parameter grids for C, γ, and scale s preventing evaluation bias.

05.

The Entanglement Deficit

Entangling CNOT gates collapse single-qubit expectations toward zero, degrading generalization by 0.17 to 0.22 AUC points.

06.

The Scale Duality

Tuning scale s to 0.0625 recovers classical performance by Taylor-expanding sin(θ) ≈ θ, rendering the quantum circuit an expensive linear map.

03 / 08
Architecture & Methodology

Transfer learning accounts for 100% of diagnostic capability.

Compressing ResNet-18 features into an 8-qubit quantum register via angle encoding maps biological lung opacities into Hilbert space. Yet without inductive bias matching anatomical structure, quantum features introduce periodicity without semantic gain.
